6. AI features

Coach PATH (players) and Assistant Coach Pickle (coaches) are AI, not human coaches. Other features also use AI, such as coach tools, coach bio drafts, goal suggestions, and smart session build.

The app asks for your consent before you first send a chat message. One consent covers every AI feature. With consent, what you write plus relevant training context is shared with our AI providers (Anthropic, and for tagging coach notes, OpenRouter or Cloudflare), as described in the Privacy Policy. If you are a coach, the context can include a selected player’s training history and your notes about them marked “Shared with AI”. Without consent, chat is unavailable and other features work without AI.

Coaches: mark a note “Shared with AI” only if it is appropriate to use it that way, and do not put a player’s health details in such a note unless the player agrees.
