Features

Your Game, Personalized

Stop guessing what to practice. PicklePath assesses where you are and builds the path to where you want to go.

Skills Assessment

Evaluate your game across 4 core skill areas. The more you assess, the more personalized your training becomes.

Personalized Training

AI-generated sessions built around your weaknesses, goals, and available time — not a one-size-fits-all plan.

Shot Mastery

Deep-dive into your comfort level with every shot in your arsenal and get targeted drills to fix gaps.

AI Coach Card

A daily adaptive insight from your personal AI coach, contextually tuned to your recent training and progress.

Progress Tracking

Watch your rating climb as you train. Visual progress, achievement milestones, and reassessment prompts keep you on track.

Mental Game Training

7 mindset categories covering pre-match prep, pressure, focus resets, confidence, and mental recovery.

The Loop

Assess. Train. Improve.

A data-driven cycle that keeps getting smarter the more you play.

1

Assess Your Skills

Take a quick quiz and full skills assessment. The app pinpoints exactly where you stand across all areas of your game.

2

Train to Improve

Get a personalized training session built from your profile. Warmups, drills, technique, games, mindset — all tailored to you.

3

Watch Your Rating Climb

Your combined rating updates as you train. Reassess periodically and watch the improvements stack up over time.
