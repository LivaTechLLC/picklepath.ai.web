Features

Training that follows
your assessment

Home, Train, Library, and Profile for every player. Coaches also get Team. Chat is always one tap away.

Combined rating

Home shows your rating. Open Assessment Hub to work through Skills (four areas) and Shot Mastery.

Goals

Set goals on Home, or ask Coach PATH to add one from chat. Progress updates when you complete sessions.

Train

Choose a focus, pick 30, 60, 90, or 120 minutes, and start. Or build a session from scratch. Notes and coaches sit on the same screen.

Coach PATH

Chat from any screen. Players talk to Coach PATH. Coaches talk to Pickle. Start a drill or draft feedback without leaving the conversation.

Library

Warmup, drills, games, stretching, technique videos, and mindset. Save favorites and add them to your next session.

Coaches and Team

Players find and connect with a coach. Coaches get a Team tab for roster, player detail, and 1:1, group, or clinic sessions.

How It Works

The loop
assess · train · reassess

Start with a short quiz. Add Skills and Shot Mastery when you are ready. Sessions get more specific as your profile fills in.

1

Assess

Complete onboarding, then Skills and Shot Mastery in Assessment Hub. Home shows your combined rating.

2

Train

Pick a focus and a duration, or browse the Library. Ask Coach PATH if you need a starting point.

3

Reassess

Finish the session. When you are ready, reassess so the next plan uses your new scores.

Two Paths

A player path and a coach path

One app, two modes. Switch when you need to. AI handles the busywork; people keep the relationship.

Player

Assess, train, set goals, and talk to Coach PATH. Connect a human coach when you want someone on the court with you.

  • Home, Train, Library, and Profile
  • Combined rating and Assessment Hub
  • Goals on Home; sessions from Train
  • Chat with Coach PATH from any screen
  • Find and connect with a human coach

Coach

The same Train and Library as players, plus Team. Ask Pickle to draft feedback or prep. You still take attendance and write the notes that matter.

  • Home, Train, Team, Library, and Profile
  • Roster, player detail, and reassessment
  • 1:1, group, or clinic sessions
  • Attendance, shared notes, and private notes
  • Chat with Pickle, with a player in context
Who we are

Built after practice,
in North Carolina

Livia Vass is a pickleball addict — here she is on pickleball.com. She wanted a training partner that knew her game, not a random drill list and not a rating that never matched how the match felt. She started building PicklePath.ai for herself.

It is still that product. Assess the gaps. Train them. Reassess. Chat with Coach PATH between sessions, or connect a human coach when you want someone on the court with you. Made with a lot of court time in North Carolina.
