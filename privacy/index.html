Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026 · Last updated [PUBLISH DATE]

This policy describes how LiVaTech Ventures LLC (“PicklePath.ai”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information in the PicklePath.ai iPhone app and on picklepath.ai. PicklePath.ai is offered in the United States to people aged 13 and older. If you live in Washington, Nevada, or Connecticut, also read our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

1. Who we are

PicklePath.ai started on the court. Livia Vass, a pickleball addict in North Carolina, built the training app she wanted for her own game: see the gaps, train them, then check the work.

LiVaTech Ventures LLC operates PicklePath.ai — the iPhone app and this website. Made in North Carolina.

Questions about this policy or your data: support@picklepath.ai.

2. Information we collect

Account and profile

  • Name and email address from Sign in with Apple or Google, and the Apple or Google account identifier that keeps you signed in
  • A PicklePath account ID we create for you
  • Profile photo, and the city and state you choose to type in (we never take your location from your device or IP address)
  • Your role in the app (player, coach, or both)
  • If you are a coach: display name, bio, specialties, location, phone number if you enter it, and an optional intro video

Age

  • Your answer to the age check when you sign up, stored as an age group (13–17 or 18 and older). If the answer shows you are under 13, we delete the account and its data right away.
  • Your birth year, if you enter it in your player profile. We use it to pick age-appropriate drills and shots.

Physical limitations (health information)

You can choose to tell us about physical limitations (knee or hip, shoulder, back, or limited mobility) and add a short note. We use this only to leave out drills, games, and shots that may not suit you. We do not send it to any AI provider, we do not show it to coaches or other users, and we do not use it for advertising. If a coach builds a session for you, the same filter applies, and the coach may see that some drills were left out. You can change or clear it at any time in Settings → Privacy → Physical Limitations. Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy covers this data in more detail.

Training and coaching content

  • Skill assessments, self-ratings, ratings history, goals, training sessions, and the drills you complete, skip, or swap
  • Notes and session feedback you write, and notes a connected coach writes about you
  • Training videos and photos you choose to upload, including the audio in those videos
  • Messages you send to the in-app AI assistants (Coach PATH for players, Assistant Coach Pickle for coaches) and their replies
  • Connection requests and the messages you include with them, reports you submit about other users’ content, and users you block
  • Your actions on mindset cards (viewed, completed, rated, or dismissed)
  • If you create a calendar link, a record of that link. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version of the link, so we can check it but can’t read it back.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold through the Apple App Store. Apple handles payment; we never receive your card or bank details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status: we give RevenueCat your PicklePath account ID, and we receive your subscription events (product, purchase, renewal, trial, and expiry dates). We store your current plan and its expiry date.

Product analytics

Product analytics are on by default. We record how you use features, such as which suggestions were shown or tapped, which conversation starters you used, and which buttons you tapped, so we can improve the app. You can turn analytics off at any time in Settings → Privacy → Analytics. When analytics are off, we stop storing this usage data. We still save a few actions the app needs in order to work the way you set it: hiding a suggestion you dismissed, how often a coach card can come back, and your mindset card progress. Analytics are first-party: we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Device and technical data

  • Error reports from our servers and from App Store builds are processed by Sentry: error details, app version, device model, and iOS version. They do not include your name or email address.
  • Server logs with your account ID, request IDs, and timing. Logs do not include email addresses and are rotated.
  • If you allow notifications on your iPhone, your device’s push token, so we can send notifications.
  • Sign-in tokens, which stay on your device in the iOS Keychain.

What we do not collect

We do not collect precise location, contacts, Apple Health data, or your advertising identifier. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

3. How we use information

  • Create and secure your account, and check that you are 13 or older
  • Run assessments, build training sessions, and show your progress
  • Let coaches and players you connect with train together
  • Provide AI features, if you agree (see section 4)
  • Manage subscriptions and access to paid features
  • Review reports and hide or remove content that breaks our Terms
  • Improve the product when analytics are on
  • Prevent abuse, fix errors, and keep the service reliable
  • Meet legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for targeted advertising or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We use your activity to personalize your training (for example, your skill level and training priorities), but we do not use profiling to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. AI features

Some features use AI: chat with Coach PATH or Assistant Coach Pickle, coach tools (session prep, feedback drafts, insights, and progress reports), AI-drafted coach bios, goal suggestions, smart session build and the quick quiz, conversation starters, chat titles, and tagging notes and goals by skill focus.

Consent. The app asks for your consent before you first send a chat message. One consent covers every AI feature. Without it, chat is not available, and every other feature still works without AI.

What we send. When you use an AI feature with consent, we send the text you write, plus relevant context: your role, skill level, training history, ratings, goals, training priorities, and notes your coach wrote about you that are marked “Shared with AI”. If you are a coach and you select a player or use coach tools, that player’s training history, ratings, goals, and your notes about them marked “Shared with AI” are included. We do not send names or email addresses to AI providers; names that you type in a message or note are sent as written. New coach notes are marked “Shared with AI” by default; a coach can change any note to private.

What we never send. Physical limitations, limitation notes, birth year, age group, videos, photos, phone number, and payment information.

Who receives it. Anthropic PBC (Claude) generates AI results. To tag coach notes by skill focus, we may also send the note text to OpenRouter or Cloudflare Workers AI. These providers process the data to return a result to us.

Training AI models. We do not collect, use, or sell personal information to train large language models. Anthropic’s commercial terms do not allow it to train its models on the data we send.

Withdrawing consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy → AI features, or by emailing support@picklepath.ai from the address on your account. From then on, chat is off and other features work without AI. Data already sent to an AI provider cannot be recalled.

AI results can be wrong. They are not medical advice.

5. What other users can see

  • Search. Any signed-in user can find you by name, or by your exact email address, and see your name, photo, and city and state if you added them. Accounts of users aged 13 to 17 do not appear in search for people they are not connected to. They can send you a connection request with a message. Your email address is never shown. You can block anyone from their profile.
  • Coach profiles. A coach’s name, photo, bio, specialties, and location appear in coach search unless the coach sets the profile to hidden. A coach’s intro video is shown only to the coach and to players with an accepted, active connection to that coach.
  • Connected coaches and players. A coach you connect with can see your training history, ratings, goals, and the notes and videos you share in that relationship. Coaches choose, note by note, whether you can see a note they write about you and whether it is shared with AI features. When a coach schedules a session with you, the app tells them if you already have a session with another coach at that time. It shows only the time, never the other session’s title or details.
  • Coach sharing (off unless you turn it on). If you connect with more than one coach, the app asks once whether your coaches may see each other’s work with you. You can change this at any time in the app. When it’s on, each of your connected coaches can see who your lead coach is, the training focus your other coaches set for you, the time and title of your upcoming sessions with your other coaches, and the notes your other coaches shared with you. Coaches never see each other’s private notes, notes kept only for AI features, or session feedback. If you’re a coach: when a player turns this on, that player’s other coaches can see your focus settings, your upcoming sessions with that player, and the notes you shared with that player.
  • Calendar link. If you create a private calendar link, anyone who has the link can see the sessions in it without signing in: the title, time and any cancellation note of your own sessions (for a coach, your sessions with your players). The calendar is named with your name. Keep the link private. You can turn it off or replace it in the app at any time, and the old link stops working right away.
  • Reports. We do not tell a user who reported them.

8. Account deletion

You can delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing support@picklepath.ai from the address on the account.

When you delete your account, we remove your name, email address, and sign-in identifiers, sign you out on every device, ask Apple to revoke Sign in with Apple for PicklePath.ai, and delete the data tied to your account: videos and photos, sessions, assessments, goals, chat history, notes, connections, analytics and engagement records, physical limitations, AI consent records, push tokens, and calendar links.

We keep only what we need for safety and the law: reports about content (so moderation decisions stand), aggregated statistics that do not identify you, and records that prevent duplicate subscription processing. Deleted data can remain in our encrypted backups until those backups expire; it is removed from backups within 6 months.

Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Cancel it in your Apple ID settings. Deleting PicklePath.ai does not delete your Apple or Google account.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account, profile, and training data: while your account is active, until you delete it or ask us to.
  • Physical limitations: until you clear them or delete your account.
  • Calendar link: until you turn it off, replace it, or delete your account.
  • Server logs and rate-limit counters: Server logs (account and request identifiers, error details) are kept for up to 90 days on systems we control, then deleted. Rate-limit counters expire on a rolling basis.
  • Crash and error reports: under Sentry’s standard retention period.
  • Encrypted backups: Encrypted backups are kept on Cloudflare R2 and on storage we control, and are fully replaced within 6 months.
  • Under-13 sign-ups: deleted right away.

10. Security

We encrypt data in transit, keep uploaded files in private storage reached only through short-lived links, encrypt backups before they leave our servers, limit staff access to what the job needs, and store sign-in tokens in the iOS Keychain. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

11. Children and teens

PicklePath.ai is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app asks your age when you sign up and deletes the account right away if you are under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email support@picklepath.ai and we will delete it.

Users aged 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian’s permission. We do not sell their data or use it for targeted advertising.

12. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, get a copy, correct it, delete it, and opt out of its sale, of targeted advertising, and of certain profiling. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising, and we use sensitive information (such as physical limitations) only to provide the service you asked for.

To make a request, email support@picklepath.ai from the address on your account. We may ask for information to confirm it is you. An authorized agent may make a request for you with your signed permission. We respond within 45 days, or tell you within that time if we need up to 45 more. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying with “Appeal” in the subject line; we will answer within 45 days and, if we still decline, tell you how to contact your state attorney general.
